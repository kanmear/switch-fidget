A standard Cherry MX switch and keycap in a low-profile housing. The opaque top has a raised collar that hides the keycap gap. The frosted lower half lights up from a small custom PCB, which also takes USB-C and reads key presses.

Circuit

The LiPo powers the MCU and LEDs at all times through the charger and regulator. The two switch contacts work as a momentary closure between a GPIO with a pull-up and GND, and the firmware turns each press into a lighting effect. USB-C charges the battery and exposes the effect settings to a config tool.
